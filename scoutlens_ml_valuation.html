Key Design Decisions
Log-scale target
log(market_value) prevents €500M stars dominating MSE. Back-transformed via exp() for reporting.
Per-90 normalization
Goals × 90 / total_minutes. Makes a 200-min fringe player comparable to a 2000-min regular starter.
Leakage removal
highest_market_value_in_eur excluded — same Transfermarkt source as target, would inflate R² artificially.
Tier 1 quota (max 40%)
Prevents Top-5 league stars dominating the list. Goal ≥60% Tier 2/3 → achieved 63%.
